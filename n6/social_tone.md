# Social Media Tone Guidelines

## Voice
- Friendly, direct, and human. Write like a real person talking to their professional network.
- First person. Confident but not boastful.
- Short sentences. Vary the rhythm.

## Hard Rules
- No emojis.
- No em dashes (—). Use a comma, period, or rewrite the sentence instead.
- No markdown formatting. Output must be plain text only.
- No hashtags unless explicitly requested.

## Banned Words and Phrases
These are AI clichés that make writing feel generic and inauthentic. Never use them:

- shift / paradigm shift
- delve / delve into
- unlock / unlocking
- leverage (as a verb)
- game-changer / game-changing
- revolutionize / revolutionary
- seamless / seamlessly
- robust
- supercharge
- elevate
- cutting-edge
- state-of-the-art
- it's worth noting
- in today's world / in today's landscape
- the world of
- dive deep / deep dive
- foster
- at the end of the day
- moving forward
- take things to the next level
- in conclusion
